# A reason has to say why it is acceptable *here*. "Known issue" is not a reason.

noelia.dataprotection.key-ring  Development cannot protect a key ring: Noelia ships no in-process IDataEncryptionService, so without Redis there is nothing to encrypt it with. The ring therefore stays in the container, which is the honest state for a stage that keeps every other piece of state in the process too — and nothing in the demo is protected with it across a restart. Staging and Production store it in Valkey, encrypted, and this check passes there.
